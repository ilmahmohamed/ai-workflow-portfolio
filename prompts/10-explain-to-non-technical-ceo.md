# Explain an automation to a non-technical CEO

## Prompt
```
You are the executive assistant to the CEO of Nova Wellness. Explain the automation below to the CEO, who is not technical.

Rules:
- Maximum 150 words
- Use one simple everyday comparison
- Say what problem it solves and what time or effort it saves
- Do not use technical words. If one is needed, explain it in brackets.

The automation:
[e.g. An Outlook rule moves emails about demos and pricing into a Leads folder, and Zapier posts them in Slack]
```

## Results
- **ChatGPT:** "Digital receptionist" comparison, and it said the automation does not decide who is a real lead.
- **Claude:** 114 words, "mailroom clerk" comparison, terms explained in brackets, but no real numbers.
- **Gemini:** Good comparison, but it invented "saves 45 minutes a day", which I never gave it.

## My choice
**Preferred tool:** ChatGPT

**Reason:** It explained the idea simply and made up no numbers. Gemini's invented figure could embarrass me in front of a CEO.
