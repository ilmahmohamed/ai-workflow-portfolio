# Slack update

## Prompt
```
Act as an executive assistant at Nova Wellness. Write a Slack update from these fictional notes. Draft it directly in this chat without accessing apps or posting anything.

Completed:
- Received quotations from two workshop suppliers.
- Checked availability with 20 staff members.

In progress:
- Comparing the quotations.

Awaiting input:
- The manager needs to approve the workshop budget by Thursday at noon.

Next:
- Recommend a supplier after completing the comparison.

Keep the update friendly, professional and under 90 words. Use four short sections: Completed, In progress, Awaiting input and Next.

Use natural wording like "I've received both quotes and am comparing them now." Don't add progress, decisions or deadlines that aren't in the notes.
```

## Results
All three tools were under 90 words and used the four sections. The results were very close.

- **ChatGPT:** Added a closing line promising to share another update, which isn't in the notes.
- **Claude:** Stuck to the notes with a short greeting and thanks.
- **Gemini:** Stuck to the notes and used bullet points under each section.
