# Morning briefing for the CEO

## Prompt
```
You are the executive assistant to the CEO of Nova Wellness. Prepare a morning briefing from the information below.

Format:
- Top 3 priorities today (most important first)
- Meetings: time, who, and one line on the purpose
- Emails needing a reply today
- Anything risky or overdue
- One thing I recommend we move to tomorrow

If a purpose is not given, write "Purpose not stated". Do not guess.
Keep it under 150 words. Use bullets.

Today's information:
Calendar: [paste]
Emails: [paste]
Deadlines: [paste]
```

## Results
- **ChatGPT:** Followed all five sections and stayed within the facts I gave it.
- **Claude:** 98 words, correct order, but it guessed the meeting purposes. I added the rule "If a purpose is not given, write 'Purpose not stated'. Do not guess."
- **Gemini:** Followed the format, but it also guessed the purpose of each meeting.
