# Draft a reply on behalf of the CEO

## Prompt
```
You are the executive assistant to the CEO of Nova Wellness. Draft a reply to the email below for the CEO to approve.

Tone: warm, professional, concise.
Rules:
- Under 100 words
- Do not promise prices, dates or decisions. Use only the facts I give you.
- If something needs the CEO's decision, put it in square brackets like [CEO to confirm]
- Sign off as the CEO's name: [CEO name]

Facts I can share:
[e.g. we offer a 30-minute demo, available Tue to Thu]

Email received:
[paste email here]
```

## Results
- **ChatGPT:** Good draft, but it only flagged availability and not the pricing decision.
- **Claude:** 75 words, no invented facts, and it put the pricing decision in brackets.
- **Gemini:** Good draft with both the time slot and pricing in brackets.

## My choice
**Preferred tool:** Claude

**Reason:** It kept to the facts I gave it and flagged every decision for the CEO. Gemini was close.
