# Meeting preparation brief

## Prompt
```
Act as an executive assistant at Nova Wellness. This is a fictional exercise. Use only the details below and write your answer in this chat without accessing connected apps.

I need to prepare my manager for a 30-minute meeting about a staff wellbeing workshop.

Notes:
- The workshop is for 20 employees.
- The proposed budget is LKR 120,000, but it hasn't been approved.
- We have received one supplier quotation and are waiting for another.
- The date and venue haven't been decided.
- The meeting needs to cover the budget, possible dates and who will follow up with the suppliers.

Create a meeting brief under 200 words with:
- Meeting purpose
- Key facts
- Decisions needed
- A suggested agenda totalling 30 minutes
- Information still to confirm

Keep the wording simple. Clearly separate proposals from confirmed facts. For example: "Budget: LKR 120,000 proposed; approval pending."
```

## Results
- **ChatGPT:** Complete brief with a 30-minute agenda table. Listed facts and proposals together, marking the budget as "proposed; approval pending".
- **Claude:** Split the key facts into "Confirmed" and "Proposed or undecided". Agenda adds up to 30 minutes.
- **Gemini:** Covered all the points, but put "Information still to confirm" before "Decisions needed".

## My choice
**Preferred tool:** Claude
