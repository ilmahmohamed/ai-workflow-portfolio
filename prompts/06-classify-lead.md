# Classify an email: lead or not a lead

## Prompt
```
You are the executive assistant to the CEO of Nova Wellness. Read the email below and decide if it is a real lead, meaning someone who wants to buy from us or book a demo.

Reply in this exact format:
Lead: Yes or No
Reason: one short sentence
Next step: what we should do (reply, ignore, or move to Inbox)

Email:
[paste email here]
```

## Results
- **ChatGPT:** Correct, exact 3-line format. Tested on a real lead only.
- **Claude:** Correct on both a real lead and a cold pitch. Suggested adding an "Unsure" option.
- **Gemini:** Correct format, but the next step added extra detail I didn't ask for.

## My choice
**Preferred tool:** Claude

**Reason:** It was tested on both a lead and a cold pitch, and it kept to the exact format.
