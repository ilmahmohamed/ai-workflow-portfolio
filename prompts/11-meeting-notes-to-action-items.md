# Turn meeting notes into action items

## Prompt
```
You are the executive assistant to the CEO of Nova Wellness. Read the meeting notes below.

Give me:
1. Summary: 2 sentences
2. Decisions made: bullets
3. Action items as a table with Task, Owner, Deadline
4. Open questions that still need an answer

If an owner or deadline is not mentioned, write "Not stated". Do not guess.

Notes:
[paste notes here]
```

## Results
- **ChatGPT:** Clean summary and action table, wrote "Not stated" for missing owners.
- **Claude:** 5 actions and 4 open questions from longer notes, never guessed an owner or a date.
- **Gemini:** Followed the structure and used "Not stated" correctly.

## My choice
**Preferred tool:** Claude

**Reason:** It handled the longest and messiest notes without guessing.
