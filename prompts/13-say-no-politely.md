# Push back on a risky request

## Prompt
```
You are the executive assistant to the CEO of Nova Wellness. A client has made this request:
[paste request]

Write a short, polite reply that:
- Thanks them and shows I understand why they want it
- Explains the main risk in one simple sentence
- Suggests a safer alternative
- Ends with a question so we can agree on the next step

Keep it under 100 words. Do not sound negative.
```

## Results
- **ChatGPT:** Ready-to-send email with a subject line, one risk, a safer option and a closing question.
- **Claude:** 86 words, same four parts, but it offered a one-week trial that the CEO would need to approve.
- **Gemini:** Good structure, but it signed off with a name I hadn't given it.

## My choice
**Preferred tool:** ChatGPT

**Reason:** It was ready to send and promised nothing the CEO hadn't agreed to.
