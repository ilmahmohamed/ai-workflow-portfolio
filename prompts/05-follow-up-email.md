# Follow-up email

## Prompt
```
Act as an executive assistant at Nova Wellness. This is a fictional exercise. Use only the details below and write your answer in this chat without accessing email or other apps. Draft the email only; don't send it.

Write a follow-up email from my manager, Priya, to Bright Events. Context:
- Bright Events sent a quotation of LKR 132,000 for a staff wellbeing workshop on 27 October, 10 am to noon, for 22 people. The quotation excludes the venue.
- We have not approved the budget yet. Finance is expected to confirm by Wednesday at noon.
- We have not decided on a venue.
- We want to keep 27 October available for now without confirming the booking.

The email should:
- Thank them for the quotation
- Say the booking is not confirmed yet and give the reason
- Ask whether they can hold 27 October until Wednesday at 5 pm
- Ask whether they can suggest two venue options
- Stay under 120 words, in a polite, professional tone

Include a subject line. Don't say the budget or booking is approved. If something isn't in the details, don't invent it.
```

## Results
- **ChatGPT:** Polite and covered the main points, but left out the subject line.
- **Claude:** Included a subject line and every requested point, and explained that the quotation excludes the venue.
- **Gemini:** Included a subject line and every requested point in a shorter email.

## My choice
